# BitLife ideas for OK Studio

Date: 2026-10-24. Written for the game's owner. Research, not a plan: nothing in here is approved.

**What this is.** You asked how the producer career could feel even more like BitLife, and what all the things we could add are. This is the whole menu: 122 ideas in nine themes, each one checked against the game as it is today.

**How it was made.** Six lenses each wrote ideas (core loop, fame and career, relationships, events and assets, the Roblox market, code hooks), a critic added twenty gaps the lenses missed, the lists were merged, and a verifier read the code for every idea: does it exist already, what it really plugs into, how big it is, what could go wrong. Where the verifier corrected a pitch, the table shows the corrected version.

**How to read effort.** S = content or data, a day or less (new cards, a new visitor, a line of config). M = a new module or UI piece (a tab, a card layout, a saved field). L = a new system (a new meter, a new scheduler path, a reset flow). Where the verifier changed the pitched size the table says so, like "M (pitched S)".

**How to read risk.** Moderation = how it reads for a young audience, plus parody risk. Economy = whether it can push the walk-in money share past the 50% ceiling (the greedy test player sits at 48.3% today) or move the Studio Two day. "Schema v5" in a verdict means a save-format bump: safe, but bundle them so you migrate once.

**Where to start.** Top 12 first, then the theme you care about, then the three waves at the end. Every idea appears exactly once: in a theme table, or under "Already in the game", or under "Not for this game". The Top 12 and the waves point back to those rows.

## Top 12

Ranked by BitLife-ness plus fit with this game, low risk on both columns, no blocker, with a push toward ideas that more than one lens asked for.

1. **Fame Tiers, Verified Tick And Fame Pops** (`fame-tiers`, M). Followers get names and a gate on cards, which is the spine every other fame idea hangs on, and it needs nothing new saved. Moment: crossing 20K, a banner reads "you're FAMOUS now. the blogger's gonna want a word".
2. **Floating Stat Deltas** (`stat-deltas`, S). Every stat move floats under its pill and the pill pops, the single most BitLife thing the screen can do, client only. Moment: "+1,500 FOLLOWERS" in blue, then "−5 AURA" in purple.
3. **Cards Gated By Your Life** (`life-gated-cards`, S). One needs/under field on cards and the deck starts fitting your career; the hacked page, the scam and the gossip page all ride it. Moment: "you're trending rn. one line from you. go".
4. **The Weird Deck** (`gap-5`, S). Absurd no-stakes visitors in BitLife's deadpan register; it pays nothing and makes the story log worth reading. Moment: "aura in a jar. real aura. twenty dollars."
5. **Day / Week Recap Card** (`period-recap`, L). Six of the seven lenses asked for it: the age-up summary with a ribbon that names your week. Moment: "YOUR WEEK · WK 43 · 2 placements · 1 hit · ribbon: CLIENT MAGNET".
6. **Career Day / Year Counter** (`career-clock`, M). A day number on the nameplate and card so a producer reads like an age. Moment: nameplate "YEAR 7 · HITMAKER · 37 FIRE".
7. **Career Story Log / Timeline** (`story-log`, M). Sixty lines of your career in order: the life log. Moment: "day 4 · LIL NAV X walked in · gave him the beat free · PLACEMENT +500 followers".
8. **Discography And Worked-With List** (`discography`, M). Every placement gets a line and artists mention who you've worked with. Moment: Charti: "i saw you did two with ice spike. she's loud. i'm louder".
9. **Offstage News** (`offstage-news`, M). Things happen to you between walk-ins with no visitor needed, the "something happened" pop-up. Moment: "YOUR BEAT'S IN A DANCE TREND · 40k people are doing the shoulder thing to it".
10. **Career-Gated Fits** (`career-fits`, M). The one physical asset pipeline that already works, filled out with things you earn. Moment: card "wearing: CUBAN · SHADES · GOLD WATCH".
11. **Lounge TV Headlines And Premieres** (`tv-headlines`, M). The room's drama stays on screen for minutes and reaches the other room. Moment: TV "BREAKING · {name} LANDS A HIT".
12. **HEAT Meter** (`heat-meter`, L). The one new meter worth its cost: it never pays money, it only decides who bothers to show up. Moment: manager: "nobody's heard a beat from you in weeks. you still doing this?"

## Not for this game

Three ideas carry a blocker. They stay listed so you can see what was considered.

- **Named Rival With A Record And A Beef** (`named-rival`, relationships, L, moderation med): "Run it back. Loser pays $200." is a money wager on a hidden roll, and the data spec already bans bet and gamble. Cut that line, or make it a fixed entry fee whose payout is not framed as winning a bet, and the rest (a named rival with a saved record, a beef settled by a cypher win) is back on the table. Also: no artist named DJ Quantize turned up, but Quantize Recordings is a real label, so pick another name.
- **Roblox Career Badges** (`roblox-badges`, assets, S): a Roblox badge needs an uploaded icon image per badge and a published place, which brushes the no-uploaded-images rule; it is your call, not a code blocker. Also the rarity percent cannot be quoted in-game, and badges no-op in Studio.
- **Weekly Challenge Board** (`weekly-challenges`, meta, L): blocked only if the prize is a Roblox badge (icon upload). As an in-game ribbon stored like the plaques there is no blocker, but the verifier re-sized it to L: the checkboxes need three events the game does not fire, a new system, a saved challenge record and a new sign.
