# Phone tour checklist: your rooms (R-T15)

Two phones, two parts. Tick each box, and send the screenshots and any notes to Claude.

## Part A: the 12-room test

Claude builds the test rooms in Studio. While the test is in, your own rooms are switched off and everyone starts in the studio.

Don't make the game public while the test is in.

- [ ] In Studio, choose File → Publish to Roblox.
- [ ] In Game Settings → Permissions, the game stays Private.
- [ ] Open the game on both phones with your own account.
- [ ] Before you start, close any card or panel that's open. If one pops up during the tour, note which plot it was in.
- [ ] Phone 1: tap **ROOM TOUR** and put the phone down until the results card shows (about 3 minutes). Screenshot the card.
- [ ] Phone 2: the same. Screenshot the card.
- [ ] The card's RESULT line says **PASS**: every plot ran at 30+ frames a second, its worst moment stayed at 20+, and it loaded within 2 seconds. If it says CHECK, send the card anyway.
- [ ] Tap **NEXT PLOT** to stand in a bedroom. The first plaque on the wall shows the longest line the game can write. Can you read it from where you stand?
- [ ] At the laptop, the "Open gear rack" bubble sits above the "Make a beat" bubble, and you can tap either without hitting the other. The rooms aren't yours, so a tap only says whose laptop it is.
- [ ] The two speakers stand on the desk, not sunk into the wall or the desk.
- [ ] Walk out the bedroom door and into the home studio and the real studio by their doors. Note any door that takes more than 2 seconds.
- [ ] In the home and real studios, every poster shows its whole word in all three styles.
- [ ] Tap **HUB** to go back to the studio.
