**Everything here is a proposal for your approval**, as in Wave 3 and the rooms copy. The build uses these drafts, and you approve after (§9). Nothing here changes `src/`: Tasks 1-6 do that.

**Decisions this doc builds on** (made before it was written; each is also a switchable row or choice in §9):
- Card 1 shows JUNO VALE's real pay (`Data/Artists.lua`, brief `j1`: `reward.cred = 400`, so `{pay}` is "$400"), not the 11-06 text's "$50", and the haggle asks `{ask}` = pay + 50 ("$450"). Why: a "$50" card would be wrong by $350 to $550, and paying only $50 would drop the session-1 floor from $625 to $75.
- Card 2 is D10 option C: it plays the beat on your own room's speakers (no airing, no FIRE), and its toast points at airing it in the studio next.
- Card 3 names the cheapest tool you can afford and names the Desk monitors (`spk_monitors`) as the next goal, not something to buy now: monitors need 200 followers and level 2, and one delivery leaves a player at about 40-120 followers and level 1.

## How to read this doc

**Every player-facing line sits in a table row** with an id (`4.8`), where it lands, its kind, and the exact text in backticks. §7 and §8 rows also carry the old text as `Was "…"`, copied from the file, so the builder can find the line when the numbers drift.

**Tokens.** Cast display names only ever come through `{name:<id>}`, and this doc needs none. JUNO VALE is a request artist from `Data/Artists`, not a walk-in roster name, so she comes through `{artist}` only (plan call 17) and no row writes her name or "juno" any other way. The money tokens carry their own `$`: never write `${pay}`.
