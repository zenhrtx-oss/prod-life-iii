## 2. The arc cards (Task 5)

Each block lists its arc's cards (for `W.Cards`) and follow-ups (for `W.Followups`). Shapes are the plan's Shared shapes:
- outcome `rel = { who?, score?, flag?, clear? }`
- card gates `needs.flags`, `under.flags`, `needs.rel` and `under.rel`

**New, by ruling: one choice field.** `beatMin` (§2.1, §9) is the only field these literals use that the plan's Shared shapes don't already name.

**`who` is left out** wherever the target is the card's own visitor (it defaults to that), and that covers every write in the new cards.

**Trigger writes on existing cards.** The design's table puts them on `rival_beat`, `mom_food`, `two_sessions` and the rest. They're Task 5's data edits and aren't repeated here, except for the one this copy adds (`mom_plaque`, §2.2).

**Once guards (ruling).**
- Seven cards are guarded: `mom_aunt`, `homekid_show`, `navx_tour`, `navx_tv`, `navx_replaced`, `sponsor_renew`, and the `blogger_expose` pair.
- Each outcome sets a flag of the card's own, and the card gates `under.flags` on it, so the card comes once.
- `sponsor_renew`'s flag lives in `rel.rep`, which a new signing resets, so it's once per deal.
- One write holds one flag, so these outcomes carry no other flag. Where an outcome also moves the score, it shares the write: `{ score = -1, flag = "show" }`.

House rules these follow (they're the data spec's):
- Bubbles are lowercase and headlines and tags are caps.
- Names come only through `{name:<id>}` and `{sponsor}`.
- A `win` or `loss` outcome carries a stat delta of its sign (`rel` is not a tag stat, so an outcome that only writes `rel` is `neutral`).
- A sure choice that pays money quotes the amount.
- Mom never costs and never loses.
- Every follow-up has a full `resolve`.
